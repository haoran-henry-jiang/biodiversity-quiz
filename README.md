🌿 Biodiversity & Habitat Loss Quiz (in jeju)

An interactive educational quiz designed to raise awareness about biodiversity, habitat destruction, and the importance of protecting ecosystems.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Raise awareness of biodiversity and its importance.

- Explain the causes and consequences of habitat loss.

- Encourage informed decisions about environmental conservation.

- Make environmental education engaging, especially for middle and high school students..


Do not communicate correct answers through colour alone.

🌏 Purpose

Biodiversity supports the ecosystems on which life depends. By making environmental knowledge more accessible, this project hopes to encourage greater awareness of habitat loss and the importance of conservation.
